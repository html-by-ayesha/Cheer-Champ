Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Cheer Champ

🛍️ Orders & Shopping

How do I place an order?
Simply browse our collections, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can pay via credit card, debit card, or cash on delivery. Create an account for faster checkout next time!
Can I modify or cancel my order?
You can modify or cancel your order within 1 hour of placement. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be changed. Contact us immediately at contact@cheerchamp.com for urgent modifications.
Do you offer international shipping?
Currently, we ship within Pakistan only. We're working on expanding internationally soon! Subscribe to our newsletter to be the first to know when we start shipping to your country.

📦 Shipping & Delivery

How long does shipping take?
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days for major cities and 5-7 business days for other areas. Express delivery (available in select cities) takes 1-2 business days for an additional fee of PKR 500.
What are the shipping charges?
Standard shipping is PKR 250 nationwide. FREE shipping on orders above PKR 5,000! Express delivery costs an additional PKR 500. Check our shipping page for more detail.
How can I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email and SMS. Use this number to track your package on our courier partner's website. You can also track orders in your account dashboard.

🔄 Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, with all tags attached. Sale items and customized shop are not eligible for returns. Visit our returns page for complete detail.
How do I initiate a return or exchange?
Contact our customer care at contact@cheerchamp.com or call +92-300-1234567 with your order number. We'll send you a return label and instructions. Pack the item securely and hand it to our courier partner who will collect it from your address.
When will I get my refund?
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive your return. The amount will be credited to your original payment method. Store credit refunds are instant and can be used immediately.

👗 shop & Sizing

How do I choose the right size?
Check our detailed size guide with measurements for all age groups. Each product page also includes specific sizing information. When in doubt, size up! Contact us for personalized sizing help.
Are your shop true to color?
We do our best to display accurate colors, but slight variations may occur due to screen settings and lighting. Product descriptions include detailed color information. If you're unsure, contact us for additional photos!
How do I care for my Cheer Champ items?
Care instructions are included with every item and on the product care label. Generally, we recommend gentle machine wash or hand wash in cold water, air drying, and ironing on low heat. Avoid bleach and harsh detergents.

👤 Account & Security

Do I need an account to shop?
No, you can shop as a guest! However, creating an account lets you track orders, save addresses, earn rewards points, and get faster checkout. Plus, you'll get exclusive early access to sales and new collections!
Is my payment information secure?
Absolutely! We use industry-standard SSL encryption to protect your data. We never store your complete card detail on our servers. All payments are processed through secure, PCI-compliant payment gateways.
How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email address. We'll send you a reset link within minutes. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or contact support@cheerchamp.com for assistance.
