📐 How to Measure

📏

Chest/Bust

Measure around the fullest part of the chest, keeping the tape parallel to the floor

⭕

Waist

Measure around the natural waistline, which is the narrowest part of the torso

📍

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of the hips, approximately 8 inches below the waist

💡 Sizing Tips

  • When in doubt, size up for a comfortable fit
  • Check individual product pages for specific measurements
  • Consider the fabric - stretchy materials offer more flexibility
  • Contact us if you need help choosing the right size
